# Product

SLARecover converts contractual SLA rights into reproducible recovery workflows.

## User-visible output contract

Every evaluated incident must be able to state deterministically:
- provider and service;
- applicable SLA version/source;
- measurement period;
- required and measured availability;
- eligible spend and currency;
- credit tier and estimated credit;
- evidence completeness;
- applied exclusions;
- claim deadline.

## Phase 0 ICP priority

1. MSPs.
2. FinOps consultants/teams.
3. High-cloud-spend companies.
4. SaaS companies.
5. Procurement/vendor management later.

## North Star

Verified Credits Recovered.
